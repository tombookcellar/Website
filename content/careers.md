Everyone who works at the BookCellar is, first and foremost, a bookseller. Whatever your role, you'll be helping customers find their next read, serving wine and coffee, and supporting our events programme. We're looking for people who love books, enjoy genuine conversation, and want to be part of building something new in Bristol.

We're currently recruiting for three roles ahead of our Spring 2026 opening. Details for how to apply can be found in the job description.

Please note that all roles involve serving alcohol, so we can only accept candidates aged 18 years and older.
