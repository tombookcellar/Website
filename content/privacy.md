Last updated: March 2026

This privacy policy explains how BookCellar Bristol Limited (trading as the BookCellar) collects and uses your personal data. We are based at 100 Stokes Croft, Bristol, and are the data controller for the purposes of UK GDPR.

### What data we collect

When you sign up to our mailing list, we collect your email address. When you visit our website, we collect anonymised usage data via Google Analytics 4, including pages visited, time on site, and general location. We do not collect any data that identifies you personally through analytics.

### How we use your data

We use your email address to send you updates about the BookCellar, including our opening, events, and offers. We use analytics data to understand how our website is being used and to improve it. We will never sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

### Who we share data with

We use Klaviyo to manage our mailing list. Your email address is stored and processed by Klaviyo in accordance with their privacy policy. We use Google Analytics 4 for website analytics. Both services may process data outside the UK; where they do, appropriate safeguards are in place.

### Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can unsubscribe from our mailing list using the link in any email we send. For any other requests, contact us at contact@bookcellar.uk.

### Cookies

Our website uses cookies to support Google Analytics 4. These are analytical cookies only. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

### Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at bookcellar.uk/privacy.
