title: "Book Clubs"

The BookCellar is opening a dedicated space for book clubs in Bristol at 100 Stokes Croft. We have private rooms designed for book groups, with wine and coffee on hand and a shop full of whatever you want to read next.

We believe Bristol deserves a proper home for reading groups. Somewhere with the right atmosphere, the right books, and a drink that makes a good conversation better. Our rooms are available for established book clubs looking for a new venue, and we will be hosting our own groups across a range of genres from literary fiction to crime, classics to debut novels.

Whether you already have a group and are looking for somewhere to meet in Bristol, or you want to join one of ours, we would love to hear from you. Sign up below to join the waitlist and tell us what you are looking for.
